ORIGIN

Simplifier started in 2022, but its roots go back to 2001. My first company never found a way to make money, and I had to watch my colleagues leave. That day, I decided to become someone who makes a business succeed, rather than just someone who builds a good service.

After that, I worked at Naver, Samsung Electronics, Yogiyo (Delivery Hero Korea), and Bithumb, growing from individual contributor to team lead, PM, CPO, and COO. Some people call it a great career. For me, it was the process of building Simplifier. I saw organizations grow from 50 to 1,000 people, at companies ranging from brand-new startups to global enterprises. I learned that every stage of growth brings different problems, and that a real solution only works once the problem is clearly defined.

That is why Simplifier practices Playing Coaching: not giving advice from the side, but doing the work together. Sometimes as a CPO, sometimes as a coach, sometimes as a speaker, I simplify problems directly in the field. Because when you clear away complexity one piece at a time, eventually there is nowhere left to go but toward growth.

Today, I am building services that accelerate startup growth, working late into the night with my team of AI agents.

FAQ

The name Simplifier means making complex problems simple so growth comes sooner. A startup's path to growth is usually tangled up in many causes. When that path becomes simple, growth actually speeds up. Simplifier creates explosive growth by making business goals and the direction of work clear. Unlike typical consulting, we build the path together with the CEO and the people doing the work.

We run two tracks. The Startup Playing Coach track is for early-stage startups in their business transition after product-market fit (PMF). We work on-site 1 to 5 days a week as CPO, PO, or Advisor and do the work alongside the team, improving product metrics and team capability at the same time. Past cases include revenue growing more than 3x and a company moving from losses of several hundred million won to break-even. The Corporate Talk track offers 1- to 3-hour talks and workshops on leadership, time management, and project management for new team leads, newly promoted managers, and employees. Each session is designed to go beyond theory and carry into real practice.

We start with a casual coffee chat to understand where your company is now and where you need coaching. Then we share similar cases Simplifier has worked on and how we actually work. Together we check whether hands-on collaboration is possible in your environment and whether our communication styles fit. Once we both agree, we sign a formal contract.
