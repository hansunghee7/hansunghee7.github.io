About

Han Sunghee

CEO of Simplifier · Hands-on Startup Coach

I build products. I started at NAVER, then worked at Samsung Electronics, Yogiyo, and Bithumb, growing from individual contributor to team lead, PM, CPO, and COO. I was there when a company grew from 50 people to 1,000, and when a 200-person organization reached KRW 1 trillion in revenue.

Today, as CEO of Simplifier, I work on site with startups and build products alongside them. I chose not to be an advisor who gives advice and leaves. I work next to founders and their teams and execute with them.

My talks and workshops come from that work. I bring the problem that stalled yesterday's meeting and the call I got wrong this quarter. So cases come before theory, and every session ends with hands-on practice.

Speaking & Workshop

What I talk about

Three topics refined together with corporate learning teams. I adjust each one to the audience and the time available.

“From PMs Who Execute to PMs Who Deliver Results”

PMs · POs · Product leaders  ·  200 min  ·  Includes practice

See the outline
  • What separates an execution PM from a results PMExecution PM vs. results PM · What C-level leaders expect from PMs · Real failures and successes
  • How to startStakeholders: read the field first · Kickoff: agree on success criteria first · WBS: design the flow, not the tasks
  • How to executeAgile: fast execution over a perfect plan · Issue management: problems grow the later you find them · Communication: moving people without authority
  • Hands-on workshopDefine a team assignment · Write a project strategy sheet · Team presentations and feedback

“From Contributor to Leader”: An Upgrade Strategy for the Newly Promoted

Employees promoted to manager level, new middle managers  ·  150 min  ·  Includes practice

See the outline
  • Why did it get harder after the promotion?What is expected after a promotion · Contributor vs. leader · Real failures and successes
  • The leader's strategyManage success, not tasks · Manage people, not work · Prepare the stage, not the meeting
  • Hands-on workshopDefine a team assignment · Write goal, stakeholder, and meeting strategy sheets · Team presentations and feedback

“Communication That Grows With Your Team”: How Successful Leaders Communicate

Newly promoted team leads and department heads, new leaders  ·  200 min  ·  Includes practice

See the outline
  • Why leaders must communicate differentlyWhy directive leaders fail · Why a shift to coaching is needed · Real failures and successes
  • Skills that help people grow1-on-1s: getting team members to speak first · Feedback conversations: grow people, do not grade them · Performance management: direction over numbers
  • Skills for running a teamDaily scrum · Weekly meetings: alignment, not reporting · Retrospectives: make team learning a routine
  • Hands-on workshopDefine a team assignment · 1-on-1 meeting simulation · Team simulations and feedback

Workshops with practice work for 10 to 100 people. Lecture-style talks with less practice work for up to 500. Sessions are in person by default, and online sessions are also available.
